# HR1_Tracker
Catalyst California's data dashboard tracking HR 1 impacts to crucial safety net services across California


## About The Project

H.R. 1 is already affecting Californians individually and at the community level. This year, many residents stand to lose their CalFresh food assistance due to the bill's work requirement reporting provisions, adding to the burden on food banks across the state. Beginning in January 2027, many individuals will also begin losing their Medi-Cal health insurance coverage for similar reasons, threatening their livelihood and straining our health care systems. The ripple effects of H.R. 1 will be felt statewide as localities lose critical health care funding, the state budget is pushed into deficit, legislators are forced to cut social services, and local health care systems are stretched to provide care to all Californians, regardless of their coverage status.

Many academic, policy, and government agencies have been sounding the alarm by producing assessments of the projected impacts H.R. 1 will have across the state. Unfortunately, this data is scattered across government reports, academic briefs, and sector-specific analyses that are difficult to find, hard to understand, and disconnected from one another. Where other organizations (such as the California Healthcare Foundation) are also attempting to aggregate similar safety-net data, ours translates the data and stories in ready-to-use tools for local and state advocacy.  

As H.R. 1 implementation kicks into high gear in 2027, we have an opportunity to build a data and policy tool that centers who is negatively harmed by H.R. 1 and solutions to reduce harm. We propose to develop a data tool that tracks impacts across counties and aggregates the data at a statewide level: 

* Safety net impacts: Total Medi-Cal and CalFresh coverage losses by county and (based on data availability) by legislative district, losses in immigrant enrollment in Medi-Cal, Medi-Cal and CalFresh losses by select demographics
* Economic impacts: Health care job losses, hospital and clinic closures, grocery store closures and revenue losses
